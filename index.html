With over 11 years of hands-on experience in photography, video content creation, and social media strategy, I know exactly what catches the eye. I don't just edit pretty clips. I look at every piece of footage through a strategic marketing lens, so the end result actually drives results, not just likes.

I work fully remote, so it doesn't matter where you are. You send me the raw footage, I turn it into polished, on-brand content, and you get it back ready to post. No travel, no logistics, no waiting around
